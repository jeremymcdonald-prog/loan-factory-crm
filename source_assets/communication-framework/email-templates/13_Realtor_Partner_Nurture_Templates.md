## EMT-057 Realtor Partner Monthly Nurture

- Template name: Realtor partner monthly nurture
- Use case: Stay in touch with Realtor partners.
- Audience: Realtor
- Recommended sender: Loan Officer
- Subject line: Quick mortgage resource for your buyers
- Required placeholders: `{{RealtorName}}`, `{{LoanOfficerName}}`, `{{PhoneNumber}}`, `{{NMLS}}`
- Optional placeholders: `{{Website}}`
- Compliance notes: Marketing-focused. Do not imply guaranteed buyer approval. Do not offer marketing support in exchange for referrals. Include compliance line.
- Follow up timing: Monthly.
- Language versions if applicable: English master; localized versions supported in file 15.

Email body:

Hi `{{RealtorName}}`,

Quick check-in. If you have buyers who need help understanding financing options, I am happy to be a resource.

As a broker, I can compare available lender options we can access instead of being limited to one bank or retail lender menu. Every buyer still needs a full review before we can talk about what fits.

Any partner marketing or buyer resource should be reviewed first and cannot be offered or provided in exchange for settlement-service referrals.

Call or text me at `{{PhoneNumber}}` if you want to talk through a scenario.

`{{LoanOfficerName}}`  
NMLS `{{NMLS}}`
