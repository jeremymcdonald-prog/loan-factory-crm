## Purpose

Use these checklists before publishing, automating, translating, or sending templates.

## Before Sending Any Email

- Correct template ID selected.
- Required placeholders populated.
- Recipient type is correct.
- Source status is confirmed.
- No private information is in the subject line.
- Compliance note reviewed.
- Stop conditions checked.

## Borrower Facing Checklist

- Clear next step.
- No unsupported approval, rate, payment, or closing promise.
- Secure portal used for sensitive documents.
- Contact path included.
- Compliance line included when appropriate.

## Realtor Facing Checklist

- Useful status without private borrower details.
- No income, credit, assets, AUS, documents, or sensitive conditions.
- Buyer-specific claims are source-backed.
- Follow-up owner is clear.

## Automation Checklist

- Trigger exists in source system.
- Delay is correct.
- Stop conditions are implemented.
- Opt-out and consent controls are active.
- Manual Only and Never Automate templates are blocked from auto-send.

## AI Retrieval Checklist

- Match by stage, audience, purpose, and urgency.
- Return template ID, subject, and compliance note.
- Include related templates for next step.
- Do not invent missing terms, rates, or approvals.

## Translation Checklist

- Required placeholders preserved.
- Compliance meaning equivalent.
- Locale terms are natural.
- Human bilingual review completed.

## Compliance Checklist

- No RESPA, UDAAP, fair lending, privacy, or advertising concern introduced.
- No guaranteed approval, closing, rate, payment, or savings language.
- Rate and Loan Estimate wording remains educational and source-backed.
- Marketing compliance line and unsubscribe handling are considered where required.
