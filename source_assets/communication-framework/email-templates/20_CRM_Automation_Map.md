## Purpose

This map translates email templates into CRM workflow controls. It is designed for manual, semi-automated, and fully automated use while preserving mortgage compliance and borrower privacy.

## Automation Policy Levels

- Manual: Human selects and sends the template.
- Semi Automated: CRM may suggest or stage the template, but a human reviews before send.
- Fully Automated: CRM may send after source trigger, prerequisites, consent, and stop conditions are checked.
- Manual Only: Human review required.
- Never Automate: Automation must not send this template.
