## Purpose

This guide separates borrower, Realtor, partner, and internal communication rules so the right information reaches the right recipient.

## External Audiences

External audiences include borrowers, past clients, Realtors, title companies, insurance agents, appraisers, and third-party partners.

External emails must be clear, minimal, privacy-safe, and source-backed.

## Internal Audiences

Internal audiences include loan officers, loan coordinators, processors, underwriters, team leaders, compliance, marketing, and operations.

Internal notes may include more operational detail but should still avoid unnecessary sensitive data.

## Privacy Rules

| Audience | Safe to share | Do not share |
|---|---|---|
| Borrower | Their own next step, status, required item, reviewed limitation | Unsupported approval, pricing, or closing promises |
| Realtor | Milestone, owner, timing, transaction item needed | Credit, income, assets, AUS, private conditions, explanations |
| Title company | Property/file status and title request | Borrower financial details not needed for title work |
| Insurance agent | Property and lender insurance need | Borrower credit/income/assets |
| Internal team | Source-backed workflow detail | Speculation or unsupported conclusions |

## Routing Rules

- Borrower questions about loan options route to the LO.
- Document questions route to the loan coordinator or processor.
- Conditions and underwriting questions route to the processor.
- Marketing copy routes to compliance/marketing review.
- Pricing, rate lock, payment, and cash-to-close questions route to the LO with compliance-safe language.

## Escalation Rules

Escalate before sending when the message involves a complaint, possible denial, adverse action, fair lending concern, RESPA concern, pricing dispute, closing delay, or public marketing claim.
