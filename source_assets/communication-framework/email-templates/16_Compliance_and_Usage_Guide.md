## Review Standard

Approve a template only if it is accurate, source-backed, conditional where required, and clearly ready for human review before borrower, Realtor, partner, or public use.

The templates are approved drafting patterns. They are not a substitute for compliance, legal, lender, disclosure, or underwriting review.

## Required Review Checklist

- No misleading claims about terms, rates, fees, payments, timelines, eligibility, savings, or costs.
- No guaranteed approval, closing, rate, payment, savings, waiver, or timeline language.
- Approved, preapproved, conditionally approved, and clear to close are used only when the file status supports that exact term.
- Conditional approval language says approval is subject to outstanding conditions, underwriting review, verification, appraisal, title, insurance, and final quality or control review as applicable.
- Rate language does not quote or imply a final rate unless backed by current pricing and required disclosures.
- Rate lock language does not say locked unless a formal lock exists.
- APR, payment, down payment, points, term, no cost, or no fee language is reviewed for required advertising disclosures.
- Loan Estimate language educates only and does not replace the official Loan Estimate.
- Appraisal, title, and insurance items are treated as real review steps, not routine formalities.
- Closing timeline language does not imply closing is guaranteed.
- Realtor and partner versions do not expose borrower private details, income, credit, documents, AUS findings, or sensitive conditions.
- Subject lines are truthful and match the message content.
- Disclaimers are clear, close to the related claim, and in the same language when applicable.
- Multilingual templates preserve the same required disclosures and do not soften compliance limits.

## Automatic Revision Or Block Items

Block or revise any template that includes:

- Guaranteed approval.
- Guaranteed closing.
- Guaranteed rate.
- Final approval without source status.
- Clear to close without source status.
- Specific rates, payments, or fees without required review.
- No cost, free, or no fees unless fully substantiated and disclosed.
- Your rate is locked without formal lock confirmation.
- Missing licensing or Equal Housing line where required.
- Borrower-sensitive details in Realtor, partner, or public templates.
- Requests to email sensitive documents instead of using a secure portal.

## Marketing And Compliance Line

Use this line when the template is marketing focused or borrower facing and appropriate:

Equal Housing Opportunity. Loan Factory, Inc. NMLS 320841. Jeremy McDonald NMLS 1195266.

Marketing emails may also require unsubscribe language, a physical mailing address, suppression-list checks, and review before public use.

## Realtor And Partner Privacy

Realtor and partner updates should be useful but privacy-safe.

Appropriate:

- Status milestone.
- Timeline expectation.
- Owner of next step.
- Property or transaction item needed.
- Whether the team is waiting on borrower, lender, title, insurance, appraisal, or internal review.

Avoid:

- Credit score or credit details.
- Income, assets, debt, employment, or documents.
- AUS findings.
- Sensitive conditions.
- Borrower explanations or private circumstances.

## Rate Payment And Loan Estimate Language

Use:

- Estimate.
- Subject to review.
- Based on information available today.
- Before treating anything as locked.
- Please review the official disclosure.

Avoid:

- Final rate.
- Final payment.
- Best rate.
- Locked without confirmation.
- Guaranteed payment.
- Guaranteed savings.

## Clear To Close Closing And Funding Language

Clear to close means the file can move into final closing steps. It does not mean funding or recording is complete.

Closing timing remains subject to closing documents, title coordination, disclosure timing, final lender checks, quality control, settlement, signing, funding authorization, and recording.

Funding is complete only when the lender authorizes it and confirmation is received.

## Multilingual Review

- Use English as the source template.
- Preserve the same compliance meaning in Vietnamese, Simplified Chinese, Colombian Spanish, and Russian.
- Keep placeholders unchanged.
- Do not translate names of disclosures in a way that changes the legal meaning.
- Keep borrower instructions short and direct.
- Use culturally natural language, not word-for-word translation.

## QA Checklist

- Every markdown file has a table of contents.
- Every table of contents link points to a real heading.
- Every template has name, use case, audience, sender, subject line, email body, required placeholders, optional placeholders, compliance notes, follow up timing, and language versions.
- No duplicate template names unless intentional by language.
- No unresolved drafting markers are left behind.
- No broken markdown tables.
- All files are valid UTF-8.
- Placeholder names match the approved placeholder list.
- Compliance notes are present for every template.
- Multilingual support covers English, Vietnamese, Simplified Chinese, Colombian Spanish, and Russian.
