## Purpose

These best practices turn the library into a practical communication operating system.

## Borrower Communication

- Answer the next likely question before the borrower asks it.
- Always say what happens next.
- Use secure portal language for documents.
- Separate received from reviewed and cleared.
- Avoid jargon unless it is explained.

## Realtor Communication

- Be fast, useful, and privacy-safe.
- Give status, timing, and next owner.
- Do not share credit, income, assets, AUS, or sensitive conditions.
- Keep partner marketing educational and reviewed.

## Processor And Coordinator Communication

- Keep requests specific.
- Tie each request to the file moving forward.
- Avoid blame when documents are wrong, missing, or unreadable.
- Confirm what is pending review versus completed.

## AI Usage

- AI may retrieve, recommend, summarize, and draft.
- AI must preserve placeholders and compliance limitations.
- AI should return the template ID and compliance notes with any suggested template.
- AI should not send or approve messages.

## Automation Usage

- Automate routine status and reminder messages only after source triggers and stop conditions are reliable.
- Use semi-automation for partner updates, marketing, and milestone language.
- Keep exception, payment, rate, cash-to-close, and delay messages manual.

## Quality Bar

A template is library-ready only when it is clear, useful, compliant, source-backed, placeholder-safe, and easy for a non-technical mortgage user to understand.
