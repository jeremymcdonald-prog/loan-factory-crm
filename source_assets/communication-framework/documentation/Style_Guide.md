# Style Guide

## Voice

Use plain English. Be direct, helpful, and human. Keep paragraphs short. Give the recipient a clear next step.

## Tone Rules

- No hype.
- No pressure language.
- No unsupported promises.
- No guaranteed approval, rate, payment, savings, score increase, closing, or timing.
- Use first person when the Loan Officer is the sender.
- Use team voice when the Loan Coordinator or Processor is the sender.

## Borrower Experience

Each template should answer the recipient's next likely question:

- What happened?
- What does it mean?
- What do I need to do?
- What happens next?
- Who should I contact if I am stuck?
