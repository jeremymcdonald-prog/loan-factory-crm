# Translation Standards

## Source Of Truth

English templates are the master source. Translations should preserve the meaning, compliance boundaries, next step, required placeholders, and sender identity.

## Required Preservation

Do not translate or alter placeholders such as `{{BorrowerName}}`, `{{LoanOfficerName}}`, `{{ApplicationLink}}`, `{{PhoneNumber}}`, or `{{NMLS}}`.

Do not remove compliance notes or conditional language.

Do not turn a review, estimate, or conditional status into a promise.

## Vietnamese Guidance

Vietnamese support is planned and indexed through the multilingual template index. Full Vietnamese versions should be reviewed by a mortgage-fluent human before live use.

## QA Checklist

- Placeholders are unchanged.
- Tone is professional and natural.
- Mortgage terminology is localized correctly.
- Compliance line is preserved where needed.
- The next step is still clear.
- No approval, rate, payment, closing, or timing guarantee was introduced.
